App for Gmail Support

By MaxCode company. Contact admin@yenpi.com.

Getting started

Open the app, add an account and sign in with Google. Internet access is required. Google or your organization may restrict embedded-browser sign-in.

Notifications

Allow notifications in macOS System Settings and enable desktop notifications in Gmail settings. Configure previews and account alerts in the app.

Accounts and Pro

Your first account is available free. Pro adds multiple accounts, the menu bar inbox, custom notifications and window lock. Open the Pro window for current purchase options or Restore Purchases. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Removing an account

Use the account removal action. This requests removal of its local website data; it does not delete messages from Google. Downloaded files remain where you saved them.

Request help

Include the app version, macOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords or private mailbox content.
