Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-04
Operated by MaxCode company

Scope

MaxCode company operates App for Gmail. This policy describes the app and its support website. Effective November 4, 2026. Contact admin@yenpi.com with privacy questions.

Gmail and Google

The app displays Gmail and Google sign-in through Apple WebKit. Gmail messages, attachments and authentication requests are handled by Google. The app does not send your Gmail mailbox to MaxCode-operated servers. Google processes information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data on your Mac

The app stores account names, email addresses, profile-photo URLs, account identifiers and preferences locally. Persistent WebKit website stores retain sign-in cookies, website storage and caches, which may contain Gmail content. The menu bar inbox reads unread message information from Google and processes it on your Mac.

Notifications may include message information in macOS Notification Center, depending on your settings. Attachments you download are saved to your chosen location. The app stores purchase-access state locally and uses Apple StoreKit to check purchases.

Purchases and other services

Apple handles in-app purchases and subscriptions. MaxCode does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Google services and external links may use their own cookies and data practices. The app does not include a developer-operated analytics or advertising SDK. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process request information to operate its hosting service.

Retention and your choices

Local account and website data persist between launches. Removing an account requests deletion of its associated WebKit data; failed website-store deletions are retried on a subsequent launch. Removing an account from this app does not delete your Google account or messages held by Google. Downloaded files, macOS notifications, backups and system-managed records may remain separately.

You can manage notification previews in app settings and notifications in macOS settings, delete downloaded files yourself, and manage subscriptions through your Apple account.

Support correspondence

If you email admin@yenpi.com, your email provider sends your address, message and any attachments to our support mailbox so we can respond. This correspondence is separate from the Gmail mailbox displayed in the app. Please avoid including passwords or unnecessary private message content. Contact the same address with questions about retention or to request deletion of support correspondence.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. For questions, contact MaxCode company at admin@yenpi.com.
