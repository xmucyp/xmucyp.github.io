Privacy Policy

Word to PDF · Effective September 23, 2026

Word to PDF processes your documents on your device. We do not receive or upload the Word documents or PDFs you create.

Information handled by the app

You choose which Word files the app can open. The app reads those files to create previews and PDFs on your device. Document contents are not sent to our servers.

The app stores settings and a list of recent files on your device so you can return to your work. This list can include file names, the time a file was opened, its location, and access information needed to reopen it. The app also stores local usage information, such as the daily document-open count, and a cached purchase-access status. You can clear the recent-file list in the app. Exported PDFs are saved to the location you choose and remain under your control.

Analytics and advertising

Word to PDF does not use our own advertising trackers or analytics service, and it does not create an account with us.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may process purchase and account information under its own privacy policy and terms. The app receives purchase entitlement information so it can provide the features you bought.

Data retention and deletion

Settings, recent-file information, usage counters, and cached purchase status remain on your device until they are cleared or the app is removed. Removing an item from the recent list does not delete the original document or exported PDF. To delete those files, use the Files app or the storage location where you saved them. Apple manages App Store transaction records under its own policies.

Children

Word to PDF is a general-purpose productivity app and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@yenpi.com.
