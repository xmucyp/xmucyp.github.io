EPS Viewer: Opener & Converter

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-15
Operated by EPS Viewer

Documents and artwork

EPS Viewer opens documents you select to render artwork, inspect fonts and colors, measure pages, and create exports. Rendering, conversion and analysis take place on your device. The app does not send your documents to us or to a conversion server.

The app supports EPS, PostScript, compatible Illustrator files, PDF, SVG, XPS and OpenXPS. SVG rendering disables page scripts and blocks network resources.

Local storage and the free allowance

The app stores preferences and converted preview caches on your device. Temporary working files may be created while rendering or exporting. Caches may be removed by the app or operating system as storage is managed.

To apply the two-files-per-day free allowance, the app computes content fingerprints locally and stores the day and fingerprints in local preferences. These fingerprints are not sent to us. Reopening the same content that day and opening bundled samples do not use another free file.

You choose where to save exports. Files saved through a cloud location or shared to another app are handled by the provider or app you choose. Exported copies remain under your control.

Purchases and restoration

Pro is offered as a weekly auto-renewable subscription or a lifetime purchase through Apple's App Store. The app uses StoreKit to check purchase entitlement and restore eligible purchases. Apple processes payments and manages App Store account and transaction information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

EPS Viewer does not require an app account and does not include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics or third-party AI services. The app privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data categories. This describes the app itself; Apple services and your selected file or sharing providers have separate privacy practices.

Support and website visits

If you email admin@yenpi.com, we receive the information you choose to send so that we can respond. Avoid including confidential documents or artwork unless necessary and you choose to share them.

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visits under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. These pages contain no analytics scripts or third-party assets.

Your choices

You can stop processing by closing the document or canceling a conversion. Manage source documents and exported copies in Finder, Files or the location where you saved them. Removing the app does not remove exports saved elsewhere.

Manage or cancel weekly subscriptions in your App Store account settings. Contact support about information you have provided by email.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as app features or data practices change. The effective date identifies this version. For privacy questions, contact admin@yenpi.com.
