Privacy Policy

MBOX Editor · Effective October 7, 2026

MBOX Editor opens and converts email archives on your device. We do not receive, upload or store your mailboxes, your messages or the files you export.

Information handled by the app

You choose which mailbox files the app can open. The app reads them on your device to list, search, display and convert messages. The original files are only read and are never modified.

To reopen mailboxes quickly, the app keeps a search index of each mailbox you open, a list of the changes you make, your settings, and the access information needed to reopen files. All of this stays on your device. Exports are saved to the location you choose (on iPhone and iPad, to the app's folder in the Files app) and remain under your control.

Network connections

The app does not send message contents, email addresses or files to us or to anyone else. It connects to the internet only in these cases:

  • Sender logos. To show a company logo next to a message, the app requests the logo for the organisation part of the sender's email domain (for example, example.com) from the logo.dev image service. Only that domain name is sent, never the address or the message. You can turn sender logos off in Settings, and then no request is made.
  • Remote content. Remote images and other content in HTML messages are blocked by default. If you choose to load them, the app fetches them from the servers named in the message, as any email app would. Those servers can see your IP address and that the content was loaded.

Analytics and advertising

MBOX Editor does not use advertising, tracking or analytics services, and it does not require an account.

Data retention and deletion

Indexes, edits and settings remain on your device until you clear them in the app or remove the app. Clearing them does not affect the original mailbox or any export. To delete those files, use Finder, the Files app or the storage location where you saved them.

Children

MBOX Editor is a general-purpose productivity app and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@yenpi.com.
