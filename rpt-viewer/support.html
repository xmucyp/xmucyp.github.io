RPT Viewer for Crystal Reports

Support

Open .rpt files on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad. Read saved report data, inspect report details, and export PDF copies.

Contact support: admin@yenpi.com

Open a report

On Mac, choose Open Report in the welcome window, use File → Open, or drag a .rpt file onto the welcome window. Recent reports appear in the right pane.

On iPhone or iPad, choose a report in the document browser or open it from Files. Files must be Crystal Reports documents; other formats can also use the .rpt extension.

Read and inspect

Use Pages to browse thumbnails and Find to search report text. On iPhone, tap Pages and Info to open the sidebar sheet. Parameters shows saved values; Info includes Summary, Data, Formulas, and Raw.

Save or share a PDF

Open a report and choose Export PDF. Select the destination in the system save interface. Use Share to send the rendered PDF through a service you choose, or Print to open the system print dialog.

Why is my report empty?

A report saved without data shows only its layout. Ask the report’s author to save a copy with data included. The app cannot connect to the original database, refresh data, or change parameter values.

Why does a report look different?

Compatibility varies by report. Some formatting, report features, or saved data may not render fully. Check any warning shown in the app. If opening fails, Copy Details provides information you can include in a support request.

Get help

Email admin@yenpi.com with your app version, device, OS version, the steps you tried, and the warning or error shown. Please avoid sending confidential reports. Use a non-sensitive sample if a sample is needed.

Report processing runs on your device. No app account is required.
