RPT Viewer for Crystal Reports

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-11
Operated by RPT Viewer for Crystal Reports

Reports and local processing

The app reads Crystal Reports .rpt files that you choose and renders their saved data on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad. Report parsing, text search, report inspection, and PDF rendering run on your device. The app does not upload report contents to us or to a conversion server and does not connect to the report’s source database.

The original report is opened read-only. Exported PDFs are saved or shared using the destination you choose.

Recent files, exports, and temporary files

On Mac, the welcome window displays recent report locations maintained by the system. Clear Recents removes that history; it does not delete the original reports. On iPhone and iPad, document browsing is provided by the system and your selected file providers.

Sharing a report creates a temporary PDF in the app’s temporary storage. Temporary files may remain until the system removes them. Exported PDFs remain in the location you choose until you delete them. Removing the app does not delete copies saved elsewhere.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

The app does not require an app account and does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics services. It does not include in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple may process App Store and device diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

Services you choose

Opening a file stored with a cloud provider, sharing a PDF, printing, or following an external link can involve services you choose through the system. Those providers process information under their own privacy practices. The app does not operate a cloud conversion service.

Support requests

If you email admin@yenpi.com, we receive your email address and the information and attachments you choose to send so that we can respond. Avoid sending confidential reports or personal data; describe the issue and use a non-sensitive sample when possible. You can contact the same address with questions about your support correspondence or a request to delete it.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, or third-party embedded assets. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs when serving the pages, under its own privacy statement.

Your choices

You choose which files to open and where to save or share PDF output. Manage or delete source files and exported copies using Finder, Files, or your storage provider. For privacy questions, contact admin@yenpi.com.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.
