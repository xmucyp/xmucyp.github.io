Support

Excel to PDF for Mac, iPhone and iPad

Need help or found a problem? Email admin@yenpi.com. Please include your device, its system version and the app version (shown in the App Store).

Which files can it convert?

Excel spreadsheets in the .xls and .xlsx formats. Files that are damaged, password-protected or in another format cannot be converted.

Are my spreadsheets uploaded?

No. Spreadsheets are converted on your device, and the app has no account and no analytics. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I convert only some pages?

Yes. In Output Settings, choose Custom under Page Range and enter the pages you want, for example 1-3, 5.

How do I make the PDF smaller or sharper?

Under PDF Quality, choose High Quality for the sharpest result, Balanced for everyday use, or Small File when size matters.

Where are my PDFs saved?

In the output folder shown in Output Settings. Use Change to pick a different folder. Under File Naming you can keep each spreadsheet's original name or set your own.

What does Pro include?

Free use converts one spreadsheet each day. Pro removes that limit and unlocks batch conversion, as a one-time lifetime purchase or a weekly subscription. Weekly subscriptions renew until you cancel them in your Apple account settings. If you already bought Pro, tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen.

Reporting a problem with a specific file

Describe the file, including where it came from and roughly how large it is, rather than attaching it. Spreadsheets often contain private data, so please do not send one unless we ask and you are comfortable doing so.
