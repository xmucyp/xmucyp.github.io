Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-17
Operated by DBF Viewer & Converter

Scope and local file processing

This policy describes DBF Viewer & Converter for Mac, iPhone and iPad and its support website. The app opens files you choose to browse database records, inspect memo fields and shapefile geometry, edit supported tables, run SQL and convert or export data.

DBF parsing, editing, conversion and SQL processing take place on your device. The app has no developer-operated upload or cloud-conversion endpoint. An app account is not required. Choosing a file from a cloud storage provider, saving to that provider, or sharing to another app is subject to the provider or app you select.

Local storage and your files

The app stores display settings, encoding preferences and recent-file information on your device. Recent items include file names, paths, access bookmarks, record and field counts and the time opened, so the app can reopen your selections. Clear Recent Files in Settings removes the app's saved recent-item list; it does not delete source documents.

Imports, exports and demo data may create temporary working files. Source documents and exported copies stay in the locations you or your storage provider choose. Edits to an existing DBF are written to that file; keep a backup before modifying important data. Closing a document does not erase source files or saved exports. Manage or delete those files in Finder, Files or your chosen storage service.

Apple Maps

When a shapefile appears to contain geographic coordinates, its Map tab can show the geometry over Apple Maps by default. MapKit may communicate with Apple to obtain map content for the displayed area; the requested map region can reflect the area covered by your shapefile. Apple handles its service data under its own privacy practices. The app does not request access to your device's current location.

Use Plain view in the Map tab to render the geometry locally without the Apple Maps basemap. Apple Maps privacy information: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/

Natural-language SQL

On compatible devices where Apple Intelligence is available, the SQL assistant uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. It supplies your question and table schema, including field and table names, to that on-device model to draft SQL. A built-in rules-based translator is used when the model is unavailable. The app does not integrate a third-party cloud AI service. Generated SQL runs against an on-device SQLite workspace.

Purchases and restoration

Optional Pro access is offered through Apple's App Store as a weekly auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. StoreKit obtains product information and verifies purchase entitlements and restoration. Apple processes payments and handles App Store account and transaction information under its own privacy policy. The app does not receive your payment card details.

Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings. Restore Purchases checks for eligible purchases associated with your Apple Account. Apple privacy information: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Advertising, tracking and analytics

The app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, a third-party analytics SDK or a developer-operated crash-reporting endpoint. These statements describe integrations in this app, not data handled separately by Apple services, file providers, apps you share with, or the support website host.

Support messages and website hosting

If you contact admin@yenpi.com, your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide are used to handle your support request. Avoid sending confidential database files when a description, redacted screenshot or non-sensitive sample will explain the problem. Contact the same address about access to, retention of, or deletion of information you supplied to support.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about site visits under its own privacy statement. These support and privacy pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising scripts or third-party assets. GitHub privacy information: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Your choices and security

Choose which files to open, where to save exports and which apps or storage providers receive copies. You can close a table, clear the recent-file list and select Plain view for geometry. Local processing reduces the need to transfer database content, but the security of your device, backups and selected storage services also matters. No method of storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

The app is a database utility for general use and does not provide social features or child accounts. It does not ask for a date of birth or request children's information.

Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date identifies this version. Contact admin@yenpi.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you supplied.
