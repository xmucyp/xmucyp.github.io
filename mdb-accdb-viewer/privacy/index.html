Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-06
Operated by MaxCode company

Scope

MaxCode company operates MDB ACCDB Viewer for Mac, iPhone and iPad. This policy describes the app and its support website.

Database files and local processing

When you open a database, the app copies it into private working storage and processes its tables on your device. Database content, including records and supported attachments, is used to browse, search, query, preview and export the data you select. The app does not send database contents to MaxCode-operated servers.

The app builds local SQLite caches of table data and a separate local snapshot for the SQL worksheet. It also stores recent-file names, paths and bookmarks, column preferences, saved views (including query text and filters), and export presets.

Storage and your choices

Working copies and export staging files use local temporary storage; normal cleanup removes working files when replaced or released, but interrupted operations may leave temporary data. Table caches persist for reuse and are not currently pruned automatically by the app. Clearing the recent-file list does not delete the original files or cached table contents.

You can remove recent-file entries, delete saved views and presets, and delete exports you no longer need. App data, system backups and exported files may remain separately. Contact support for guidance on removing local cache data. The app does not provide a secure-erasure guarantee.

Exports and system services

Exports are written to a location you choose. If you choose iCloud Drive or another cloud file provider, that provider handles storage and transfers under its own policies. Clipboard contents are handled by Apple system services and may be available to other apps or devices according to your settings.

The app has no account sign-in, advertising SDK, developer analytics SDK or in-app purchases. Apple may process App Store transactions and diagnostics according to your Apple settings and its policies.

Support correspondence and website

If you email admin@yenpi.com, your email address, message and attachments are sent to our support mailbox so we can respond. Please do not send sensitive databases, passwords or unnecessary personal information. Contact the same address with questions about retention or to request deletion of support correspondence.

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. These pages do not embed advertising, analytics scripts or third-party fonts.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The effective date identifies this version. For privacy questions, contact MaxCode company at admin@yenpi.com.
